App information and privacy policy

QR Create & Scan

A colorful QR and barcode creator with a built-in scanner.

Short Description

Create colorful QR codes and barcodes, then scan from camera or gallery.

Recommended for Google Play short description: concise, clear, and under 80 characters.

App Overview

QR Create & Scan helps you turn links, text, WiFi credentials, email details, and phone numbers into creative QR designs. Customize colors, shapes, captions, logos, backgrounds, frames, and canvas sizes, then save or share your design as an image. Save local drafts, restore recent exports, and scan QR codes or barcodes from the camera or an image selected from your device.

Long Description

Create QR codes that look fun, bright, and personal. QR Create & Scan is a creative QR and barcode studio for links, text, WiFi, email, phone numbers, and more.

Customize your QR code with colorful styles, shapes, captions, draggable labels, adjustable logos, image backgrounds, frames, and export templates. Export your design to your gallery, share it with friends or customers, save local drafts for later editing, and use the built-in scanner to read QR codes and barcodes from your camera or an image selected from your device.

Features:

  • Create QR codes for links, text, WiFi, email, and phone numbers.
  • Create Code 128 and EAN-13 barcodes.
  • Customize QR colors, shapes, accent borders, captions, labels, logos, frames, canvas sizes, and backgrounds.
  • Drag QR, caption, label, and logo layers directly on the canvas.
  • Save local drafts, automatically restore your active design, and reopen recent export history.
  • Export QR artwork as PNG or JPG images, including optional transparent PNG backgrounds.
  • Share generated QR designs as images.
  • Scan QR codes and barcodes with your camera or from an image selected from your device.
  • Paste clipboard text into the active content field or clear content fields quickly.
  • Remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase.

QR Create & Scan is designed for creators, students, small businesses, social media profiles, events, menus, and anyone who wants a QR code that feels more personal than a plain black-and-white square.

Permissions Requested

The app may request or include the following Android permissions directly or through included SDKs:

Permission Purpose
CAMERA Used for QR and barcode scanning. The camera is opened only when using the Scan feature.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE with maxSdkVersion="28" Used only on Android 8.1 and below to save exported QR artwork to the device gallery.
INTERNET and network state permissions May be included by ad, billing, or web-related SDKs to load ads, complete billing operations, and access Google services.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID May be included by Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and measurement. Google Play notes that some SDKs, including Google Mobile Ads, may declare this permission in their library manifest.
com.android.vending.BILLING Used by Google Play Billing for the Remove Ads in-app purchase.

The app does not request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. Clipboard Paste does not require an Android permission, and the app reads clipboard text only after you press Paste. The Android system image picker grants access only to the image you select for a logo, background, or scan-from-image action.

Local Storage and Retention

QR content, selected images, clipboard text pasted at your request, and generated artwork are primarily handled on your device. Exported images remain in your gallery until you delete them. Temporary export files may be stored in app cache or temporary directories and may be removed by the operating system or when app data is cleared.

The app stores local autosave snapshots, Drafts, and recent export History metadata using app-local storage. Compact thumbnail images for Drafts and History may be stored in the app documents directory. The app limits retained local history and cleans thumbnail files that are no longer referenced. This information remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.

The app may store a local Remove Ads state to keep ads hidden after a successful purchase. Google Play Billing may also provide purchase restoration information through Google Play services.

Data Deletion

You can delete app-related local data by:

  • Deleting exported QR images from your device gallery.
  • Deleting individual Drafts or History items inside the app.
  • Clearing export History inside the app.
  • Clearing the app cache or app storage in Android Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app from your device.
  • Contacting the developer at minhthang0792@gmail.com for privacy-related questions.

Purchases are managed by Google Play. If you reinstall the app, use Restore Purchase to recover Remove Ads when available for your Google account.
