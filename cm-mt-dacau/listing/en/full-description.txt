How many kick-ups can you do? 🏸

Shuttle Kick brings the shuttlecock game played on every Vietnamese schoolyard and sidewalk to your phone. The feathered shuttlecock with its rubber base falls slowly as the feathers catch the air, flips at the top of its flight and drops base first. Your job: tap or swipe at the right moment as it reaches your foot, and keep it up as long as you can.

🦶 4 KICKS, 4 GESTURES
• Tap: Inside kick, the basic kick.
• Swipe up: High kick, sends the shuttle sky high.
• Swipe sideways: Outside kick, pushes the shuttle left or right.
• Swipe down: Heel kick behind your back, the hardest and highest scoring.
Your player runs to the shuttle on their own; you only pick the kick and the timing. Swipe anywhere on the screen.

🔥 COMBOS AND SLOW MOTION
• Hit the sweet spot for a “Perfect!” and double points; too early is a “Miss!”.
• Use a kick that differs from your last two to raise the combo, up to ×5. Repeat the same kick and it drops back to ×1.
• Reach ×5 or land a perfect heel kick and the game slows down for a moment.

🍃 WIND AND RISING TEMPO
• The first 10 kicks are calm so you can warm up.
• Every 10 kicks the wind shifts and gets stronger, and the shuttle falls faster.
• Wind drifts the shuttle toward the edge: use Outside kicks to bring it back.
• Confetti at 25, 50 and every 100 kicks.

🛍️ SHOP AND BADGES
• Earn coins every round from your kicks and score.
• 8 shuttlecocks: white feathers, rainbow feathers, green plastic, red and brass, peacock, fire, golden, jade.
• 4 courts: sidewalk, schoolyard, park, lantern street.
• 6 trails: chalk dust, flying leaves, sparks, rainbow, flames.
• 15 badges, from 10 kicks all the way to 500 kicks in one round, each with a coin bonus.
Shop items are cosmetic only and never make the game easier: your best score is all yours.
