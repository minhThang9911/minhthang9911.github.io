Aura Daily Wall

App Introduction

Aura Daily Wall provides a curated collection of high-quality AI-generated wallpapers for Android phones and tablets. The app is designed for fast visual browsing, reliable wallpaper setting, local favorites, wallpaper history, optional daily recommendations, and an auto-change feature powered by Android background work.

Fresh visual library

Browse daily, trending, latest, category-based, and searchable wallpaper collections delivered through the app backend.

Wallpaper tools

Preview wallpapers, set them on the home screen, lock screen, or both, and download selected images where supported.

Personal controls

Save favorites, review wallpaper history, manage cache, enable notifications, and configure automatic wallpaper changes.

Long description

Transform your screen with Aura Daily Wall, a polished Android wallpaper app built for people who want fresh, artistic, and high-quality AI-generated wallpapers every day. Explore a curated visual library across styles such as nature, space, abstract art, minimalist scenes, vibrant color moods, and futuristic compositions. Every wallpaper is selected for modern mobile screens and delivered with fast thumbnails for smooth browsing. Key features: - AI-generated wallpaper collections with a fresh, modern look. - Daily, trending, latest, category, and search discovery. - Full-screen preview for checking how a wallpaper feels before applying it. - Set wallpapers to the home screen, lock screen, or both. - Save favorites locally and revisit your wallpaper history. - Optional auto-change wallpaper scheduling for a refreshed device. - Optional daily wallpaper notifications. - Cache controls to help manage local storage. - Premium upgrade option for an ad-free experience. Aura Daily Wall does not require an account. Your favorites, history, and app settings are stored locally on your device. They are not intended to represent real people, private events, or real-world endorsements. Any resemblance to real persons, places, or events is coincidental.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aura Daily Wall ("we", "our", or "the app") accesses, collects, uses, shares, retains, and deletes data. This policy applies to the Aura Daily Wall Android application with package name aura.daily.wall.

Aura Daily Wall does not require users to create an account. The app stores favorites, wallpaper history, and settings locally on the device. The free version uses third-party advertising SDKs, which may process device identifiers and usage data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and mediation.

Data We Collect or Process

  • Wallpaper and app usage data: wallpaper IDs, categories, search requests, selected wallpapers, favorites, and wallpaper history may be processed locally to provide app features. Favorites and history are stored in the local Room database on your device.
  • Settings data: notification preference, auto-change schedule, wallpaper target, separate lock screen setting, dynamic color setting, premium status cache, and related preferences are stored locally using Android DataStore.
  • Network and technical data: when the app connects to the wallpaper API or image storage, technical request data such as IP address, user agent, request time, and requested URLs may be processed by the hosting provider for security, reliability, and delivery.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob and mediation partners may collect or receive device identifiers, Advertising ID, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, diagnostic data, and other data described in their own policies.
  • Purchase data: Google Play Billing processes purchases. The app receives purchase state information needed to unlock premium or download features, but we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Notification data: if notifications are enabled, the app may schedule and display local daily wallpaper reminders. The app does not use notifications to collect contacts, SMS, call logs, or precise location.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, messages, camera, microphone, or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal and sensitive user data.
  • We do not upload your local favorites, history, or settings to an app account, because the app currently has no account system.

How We Use Data

  • To load wallpaper lists, categories, thumbnails, and full-size wallpaper files.
  • To set selected images as Android wallpapers.
  • To save favorites and wallpaper history locally on your device.
  • To support search, preview, download, cache management, and auto-change wallpaper features.
  • To send optional wallpaper notifications when enabled by the user.
  • To show ads in the free version, measure ad performance, prevent abuse, and support Start.io mediation through Google AdMob.
  • To process in-app purchases and restore premium or paid download entitlements through Google Play Billing.
  • To secure, monitor, and improve service reliability.

Android Permissions Used

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Loads wallpaper metadata, thumbnail images, full-size wallpaper files, ads, billing-related services, and remote resources.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks network availability to load content and ads more reliably.
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Supports network-state awareness used by app services and integrated SDKs.
SET_WALLPAPER Allows the app to apply a selected image as the device wallpaper.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Allows scheduled wallpaper rotation or related background work to continue after the device restarts, when enabled.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Allows optional daily wallpaper reminders and status notifications on Android versions that require runtime notification permission.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and below only Allows saving downloaded wallpapers to shared device storage on older Android versions.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Allows advertising SDKs to access the Android Advertising ID for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention, and mediation where permitted by user settings and law.
com.android.vending.BILLING Enables Google Play Billing for premium and paid download features.

Data Sharing

We share data only as needed to operate the app, provide advertising, process purchases, host content, prevent fraud or abuse, comply with law, or protect the app and users. This may include Google, Start.io, Cloudflare, Google Play services, and infrastructure providers. We do not sell personal and sensitive user data.

Data Retention

  • Local favorites, history, and settings remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Cached images remain on your device until cleared by the app, Android storage management, or the user.
  • Advertising, billing, and infrastructure providers may retain data according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.
  • Temporary server and network logs may be retained for security, debugging, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

Data Deletion and User Choices

  • You can delete local app data by using Android system settings, clearing app storage, clearing cache, or uninstalling Aura Daily Wall.
  • You can remove favorites inside the app where the favorite feature is available.
  • You can disable notifications in the app settings or Android notification settings.
  • You can reset or limit the Android Advertising ID in Android privacy settings where supported.
  • Because the app does not currently provide user accounts, there is no app account deletion flow.
