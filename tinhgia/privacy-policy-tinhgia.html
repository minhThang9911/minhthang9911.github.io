Price Compare (Tinh Gia)

A simple Android app for comparing products by real unit price, discounts, quantities, and unit conversions.

Package: co.myheart.tinhgia Developer email: minhthang0792@gmail.com Last updated: May 15, 2026

App Introduction

Price Compare (Tinh Gia) helps shoppers compare products by the actual price per unit instead of relying only on the shelf price. The app is useful when products have different package sizes, weights, volumes, discounts, or quantities.

The app works locally on the user's device. Users can enter product prices, units, discounts, quantities, and unit types, then the app calculates comparable unit prices and highlights the best value.

Long description

Price Compare (Tinh Gia) helps you compare products by their real unit price, so you can choose the better value faster while shopping.

When products have different sizes, weights, volumes, discounts, or package quantities, the lowest shelf price is not always the best deal. This app lets you enter each item's price and unit details, then calculates the comparable unit price clearly.

Key features

  • Compare multiple items side by side.
  • Automatically highlight the best price.
  • Calculate unit price from price, units, quantity, and discount.
  • Support weight comparison with kg/g conversion.
  • Support volume comparison with L/mL conversion.
  • Use "Other" units for pieces, packs, boxes, or custom comparisons.
  • Optional discount input for sale items.
  • Show or hide total price.
  • Save the current comparison locally on the device.
  • Clear all items when starting a new comparison.
  • Simple, modern interface with large controls.
  • Optional one-time purchase to remove ads.

Useful for

  • Grocery shopping.
  • Comparing bulk packs and small packs.
  • Checking sale prices and discounts.
  • Comparing products sold in kg, g, L, mL, pieces, packs, or boxes.
  • Making faster shopping decisions without manual calculation.

How it works

  1. Add the products you want to compare.
  2. Enter the price, units, quantity, discount, and unit type.
  3. Choose kg/g, L/mL, or Other.
  4. The app calculates the comparable unit price and marks the best value.

Your entered comparison data is stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not upload your entered price comparison data to our own server.
