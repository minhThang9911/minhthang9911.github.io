Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Sim Phong Thủy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Phone Numbers: When you use our Feng Shui lookup feature, you may enter phone numbers. These numbers are processed locally on your device and are NOT transmitted to our servers.
  • Search History: Your lookup history is stored locally on your device using browser storage for your convenience.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: We may collect information about your device, including device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics purposes.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the App, such as features accessed and time spent in the App.

Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect:

  • Purchase history
  • Device identifiers
  • App usage data

For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

StartApp Ads

For non-premium users, we display advertisements through StartApp. StartApp may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Location data (approximate)

For more information, see StartApp's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Services

Our App uses Google Play Services for in-app billing. Google may collect data as described in their Privacy Policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Display relevant advertisements (for non-premium users)
  • Improve and optimize the App
  • Analyze usage patterns

Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: Your search history and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • No Server Storage: We do not store your personal data on external servers.
  • Security: We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information.

Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View your data stored in the App
  • Delete: Clear your search history at any time through the Settings menu
  • Opt-out: Upgrade to Premium to remove advertisements

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
