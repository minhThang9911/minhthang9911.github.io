About this app

A 24-mountain luopan compass with Bát Trạch: enter your birth year and instantly see your lucky and unlucky directions.

Short description (Google Play)
24-mountain feng shui compass with lucky directions by your birth year.
Long description (Google Play)

Feng Shui Compass renders the full traditional 24 mountains. Enter your birth year and gender and the app computes your Bát Trạch cung phi, coloring your four auspicious directions green (Sinh Khí, Thiên Y, Diên Niên, Phục Vị) and four inauspicious ones red, right on the dial. Includes direction lock and magnetic-interference warnings. Cultural folklore content, for reference only.
