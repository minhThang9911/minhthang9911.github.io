Scooter Stack is a physics stacking game inspired by the overloaded motorbikes of Vietnamese streets: rice sacks, foam boxes, a chicken cage, a kumquat tree, a mini fridge… everything has to fit on one little scooter and reach the customer in one piece.

🛵 HOW TO PLAY
• Load up: drag to move each item, rotate it, lift your finger to drop. Real physics: stack it crooked and it falls!
• Strap it: you only have a few straps, so choose which items to tie down.
• Ride: hold Gas and Brake to get over potholes, speed bumps, rail tracks, bridges and hills. Go too fast and your cargo goes flying.

📦 16 QUIRKY ITEMS
Every item behaves differently: a rolling tire, a soft mattress, a heavy 20-litre water jug, fragile eggs and glass, a wobbly stack of plastic chairs, a durian basket, sugarcane, a block of ice…

⭐ ON-TIME TIPS
Deliver everything on time for 3 stars and a tip. Drop something on the way and the customer pays less.

🔧 GARAGE
Spend coins on more straps, strong straps, a long board and suspension. A better bike means a taller stack.

✨ FEATURES
• Endless orders with rising difficulty
• Plays offline, no account needed
• Vietnamese and English
• About a minute per order
