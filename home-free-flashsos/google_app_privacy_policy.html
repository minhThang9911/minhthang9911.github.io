About this app

A one-tap flashlight with an emergency morse SOS mode, strobe and screen light — including a Quick Settings tile.

Short description (Google Play)
One-tap torch, morse SOS, strobe & screen light. Quick Settings tile.
Long description (Google Play)

Flashlight SOS keeps it simple: one-tap torch, a standard morse SOS mode for emergencies, a 1–15Hz strobe, and a maximum-brightness white screen for close-up light. Add the Flashlight tile to Quick Settings to toggle without opening the app. No camera permission required.
