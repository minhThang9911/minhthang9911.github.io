Remember drawing a board in chalk on the courtyard bricks and playing with pebbles all afternoon? 🪨

Mancala: O An Quan & Kalah brings O An Quan, the traditional Vietnamese mancala board game, to your phone with the real folk rules: two rows of five squares, a half-moon mandarin square at each end, sowing stone by stone, chain sowing, chain captures and capturing the mandarins. Play the computer at 4 levels, or lay your phone flat between two players.

🎯 AUTHENTIC O AN QUAN RULES
• Pick one of your squares and sow left or right.
• If the next square has stones, pick them up and keep sowing; an empty square followed by stones is a capture, and captures can chain.
• A mandarin is worth 10 points (5 optional), with an optional “young mandarin” rule.
• Out of stones? Re-sow from your captures, or borrow from your opponent.
• When both mandarins are gone, collect the board and count: most points wins.

👴 4 VILLAGE OPPONENTS
• Little Tí: just learning, perfect for your first games.
• Sister Mơ: knows the basics.
• Uncle Tư: loves chain captures, punishes every slip.
• Master Đồ: the village master who thinks several moves ahead.
Beat each opponent without hints to earn a red seal and bonus coins. 8 seals across both rule sets.

👫 2 PLAYERS ON ONE PHONE
Each player owns a row; the far player’s panel is flipped so both of you can read it. Choose who goes first or alternate every game.

📖 LEARN IN ONE MINUTE
An interactive step-by-step lesson lets you try every situation: chain sowing, chain captures, mandarin captures, losing a turn, re-sowing. Full rules are always in the menu.
